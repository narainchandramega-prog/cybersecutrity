🎬 Image & video authenticity awareness

No reliable authenticity verdict can be made from appearance alone. Use independent verification.

Image / video review checklist

What to verify

  • Find the earliest known source and publication date.
  • Check whether reputable independent outlets confirm the event.
  • Look for context mismatch, edits or recycled footage.
  • Contact the person through a known, separate channel.
  • Do not treat visual glitches or AI-detector scores as proof.

OTP & password safety

Link safety

🚨 Fraud awareness alert centre

General safety notices. This page does not provide a live emergency feed.

  • Impersonation: Verify unexpected calls claiming to be from banks or officials.
  • Payment pressure: Pause if someone demands immediate transfers or secrecy.
  • Account warnings: Visit the official app or website independently instead of using message links.
  • Fake support: Do not install remote-access apps at the request of an unknown caller.

🆘 Emergency Help Centre — India

If you suspect financial cyber fraud, act quickly. Use official channels and tell a trusted adult or responsible person.

Call 1930

India's cyber financial fraud helpline. Contact your bank/payment provider immediately too.

Report online

Protect accounts

From a trusted device, contact the provider, change affected passwords, sign out other sessions and enable multi-factor authentication.

Keep records

Preserve transaction references, dates, messages and report acknowledgements. Do not share OTPs or pay anyone promising guaranteed recovery.

For immediate physical danger, contact local emergency services. CyberShield is an educational guide and cannot file a report or recover funds for you.
